About

I am an MPhil student in the Department of Linguistics at the University of Ghana, Legon. I want to understand how syntax, phonology and computational linguistics relate to one another, especially in lesser-studied languages such as Larteh, one of the languages I speak.

I finished my B.A. in Linguistics and History in 2023 with First Class honors and a final GPA of 3.85 out of 4.0. As a teaching and research assistant in the department, I ran tutorials, marked quizzes and helped supervise undergraduate research in courses from phonetics to syntax. I'm also working as a junior web developer.

My training is in linguistic theory and research methods, and I'm comfortable in the field, collecting data. I'm also interested in using computational tools to analyze linguistic structure. What I hope to do is contribute to the documentation and understanding of language diversity, and to the growth of computational linguistics.

How I study a language

  1. Fieldwork and data collection. Where it starts.
  2. Linguistic theory and research methods. The foundation.
  3. Analysis of sound patterns and sentence structure. Phonology and syntax.
  4. Computational tools to analyze the structures.

The goal: a language better documented, and better understood.

Keywords syntax · phonology · phonetics · computational linguistics · Larteh · language documentation · fieldwork

Research interests

i.

Larteh and lesser-studied languages

Larteh is one of the languages I speak, and it's where my research starts. I want to help document lesser-studied languages like it and understand how their sounds and structures work.

ii.

The syntax–phonology interface

How sentence structure and sound patterns relate to each other. I've worked on both sides as a TA, from Phonetics and Phonological Theory to Syntax I and II.

iii.

Computational linguistics

Using computational tools to analyze linguistic structures, and helping move the field of computational linguistics forward.

iv.

Fieldwork and data collection

Good analysis depends on good data. Fieldwork and data collection are the parts of research I'm most confident in, and they feed everything else: the theory, the description and the computational work.
